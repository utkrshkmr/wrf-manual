Every fire is already in the shared folder with its record, its checked met_em files and (for the four we ran) the spec it ran with. wrf-card FIRE prints its full card from those files.

A new fire

Start from the closest of these: wrf-card CANYON_2025 > my.card for a small fire, wrf-card EATON_2025 > my.card for a large one. Change the name, times, ignition and domains, keep base_spec for the lab's physics, and follow the quick start.
